Why we run this race together

One field. One family. One purpose.

The ESF Sports Meet brings church youth, families and friends onto one field for a single day of friendly competition — because fellowship isn't only built in the pews, it's built shoulder to shoulder on a track, mid-sprint, cheering for the runner in the next lane.

🎯

Purpose

A day set apart to gather every generation of the church family around one shared goal.

🤝

Unity

Different churches, one team spirit — playing side by side as one body.

🫂

Fellowship

Conversations, laughter and encouragement that outlast every scoreline.

🏅

Sportsmanship

Friendly, fair play where the referee's call is respected and every effort is applauded.

✝️

Faith

Every race run and every match played in a spirit of gratitude and grace.

Six events, one scoreboard

Pick your lane

Tap a card to read the full rules, or head straight to registration.

No. 01

Futsal 7 vs 7

Girls, boys & parents welcome. Two 15-minute halves of fast-paced, small-sided football.

  • 7 players + substitutes per side
  • 2 halves × 15 minutes, 5-minute break
  • Unlimited substitutions
  • Respect players, referees & others
  • Referee's decision is final
  • No rough play — play with heart
Register for Futsal
No. 02

Tug of War 10 vs 10

Parents vs youth — pull together, stand stronger. Best of three rounds.

  • 10 players per team
  • Best of 3 rounds
  • Referee's decision is final
  • Fair play & respect everyone
  • No rough play — play with heart
Register for Tug of War
No. 03

Relay Race 4 × team

Open for all beginners. Run together, cheer together, win together.

  • 4 players per team
  • Baton handover in the zone
  • Complete the full track
  • Fair play & respect others
  • Referee's decision is final
  • Have fun & encourage each other
Register for Relay Race
No. 04

Dodgeball 7 vs 7

Boys, girls or mixed teams. Throw it, dodge it, own it.

  • 7 players per team
  • 2 halves × 10 minutes, 2-minute break
  • No headshots, no rough play
  • Catch = out, thrower = safe
  • Stay behind the boundary line
  • Referee's decision is final
Register for Dodgeball
No. 05

Sack Race Kids · Women · Men

Jump, hop, laugh and win. One jump at a time, lots of smiles along the way.

  • 1 participant per race
  • Hold the sack at waist level
  • Hop from start to finish line
  • No falling on purpose
  • Referee's decision is final
  • Have fun & encourage others
Register for Sack Race
No. 06

100-Meter Sprint Kids · Ladies · Men

Run fast, give your best, enjoy the race — one straight-track dash to the line.

  • Age categories: Kids, Ladies & Men
  • 100 meter straight track race
  • Start behind the starting line
  • Lane discipline must be maintained
  • No false start (1 warning, next = disqualified)
  • Referee's decision is final
Register for 100m Sprint
